Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to rent a car in Cape Town?

Prices vary depending on the vehicle, rental company, travel dates, pickup location and rental duration. Comparing options before booking can help you find a suitable price.

Is it worth renting a car in Cape Town?

A rental car can be useful for exploring Cape Town, Cape Point, the Cape Peninsula, the Cape Winelands and surrounding destinations.

Where can I rent a car in Cape Town?

You can compare rental cars online and select a suitable pickup location, including options around Cape Town International Airport.

What type of car is best for Cape Town?

Economy and compact cars can work well for city travel, while SUVs and larger vehicles may be useful for families and longer road trips.

Can I pick up a rental car at Cape Town Airport?

Many rental providers offer vehicles at or around Cape Town International Airport. Check available pickup locations when comparing cars.
