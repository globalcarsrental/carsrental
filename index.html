Car Rental Guide

Everything You Should Know Before Renting

Understand the basics of renting a car so you can make better travel decisions.

How to Rent a Car

Start by choosing your destination and travel dates. Compare available vehicles, review the rental conditions and select the option that best fits your journey.

How to Compare Rental Cars

Look beyond the headline price. Consider vehicle size, mileage, fuel policy, deposit, insurance conditions, cancellation rules and additional fees.

What to Check Before Booking

Review the driver's requirements, rental location, payment rules, required documents, cancellation policy and pickup instructions.

Understanding Rental Car Insurance

Rental insurance can vary between providers and countries. Understand what is included, what is excluded and whether a security deposit or excess applies.

Fuel Policies Explained

Common policies include full-to-full and prepaid fuel. Check the agreement carefully so you understand how fuel charges are handled.

Deposit and Payment Information

Some rental companies require a security deposit or payment card authorization. Check the accepted payment methods and deposit requirements before traveling.

Picking Up and Returning a Rental Car

Inspect the vehicle carefully, photograph its condition and confirm the fuel level and mileage. Follow the return instructions provided by the rental company.

Tips for Saving Money on Car Rentals

Compare different vehicles, check multiple pickup locations, understand additional fees and book early when possible.

Travel Tips

Make Your Road Trip Easier

A little preparation can make your rental car experience smoother.

Check the Pickup Location

Airport rental desks can be convenient, but other city locations may sometimes offer different rates or conditions.

Inspect Before Driving

Walk around the vehicle before leaving and document any existing scratches, dents or other visible damage.

Read the Rental Terms

Understanding the agreement can help you avoid surprises involving fuel, mileage, deposits and additional drivers.
