New York Car Rental Tips

Important things to consider before confirming your rental.

  • Compare rental prices before booking.
  • Check the rental company's pickup and return requirements.
  • Review available insurance options carefully.
  • Understand the fuel policy.
  • Check whether mileage restrictions apply.
  • Consider parking costs when driving in New York City.
  • Check toll requirements for your planned routes.
  • Make sure your driving documents meet the rental requirements.
  • Check the vehicle condition before leaving the rental location.
  • Review the final rental terms before completing your booking.
