Rome Driving Tips

Helpful information before you start driving in Rome and Italy.

Understand ZTL Zones

Rome has restricted traffic zones known as ZTLs. Check current restrictions before driving into central areas.

Check Parking Rules

Parking availability and restrictions vary by area. Always check signs and local regulations.

Choose the Right Vehicle

Consider vehicle size, luggage space and transmission type when selecting your rental.

Review Rental Conditions

Before booking, carefully review insurance, deposit, mileage and fuel policies.
