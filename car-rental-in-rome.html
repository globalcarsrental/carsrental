Driving in Rome

Driving in Rome can be different from driving in smaller Italian towns. Traffic, parking availability and narrow streets should be considered when planning your journey.

Rome also has restricted traffic zones known as ZTL areas. Always check current local regulations and signs before entering restricted areas.

If your main goal is sightseeing in central Rome, consider whether you actually need a vehicle for the entire trip. A rental car can be particularly useful when exploring destinations outside the city.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it worth renting a car in Rome?

A rental car can be useful if you plan to explore destinations outside central Rome. For central city sightseeing, consider traffic, parking and restricted traffic zones.

Where can I pick up a rental car in Rome?

Common pickup locations include Rome Fiumicino Airport, Rome Ciampino Airport and selected city rental locations.

Can I rent a car at Rome Fiumicino Airport?

Yes. Rome Fiumicino Airport has rental car facilities serving travelers arriving in the city.

What type of car is best for driving around Rome?

A compact vehicle can be convenient for navigating smaller roads, while SUVs or larger vehicles may be better suited to families and longer road trips.

Can I drive from Rome to other Italian cities?

Yes. Rome is a useful starting point for road trips to destinations such as Florence, Tuscany, Naples and Siena. Check your rental agreement before traveling.
