Driving in Paris: What Travelers Should Know

Plan ahead and understand the practical considerations before picking up a rental car.

Traffic

Paris can experience heavy traffic, particularly during busy periods. Allow additional travel time.

Parking

Parking in central Paris can be challenging. Research parking arrangements before driving into the city.

Road Rules

Pay attention to French road signs, speed limits and local driving requirements.

Tolls

Some French motorways include tolls. Consider this when planning longer road trips.

Navigation

A reliable navigation system can make it easier to travel through Paris and surrounding regions.

Rental Insurance

Carefully review the rental agreement, coverage, excess and vehicle condition before driving away.
