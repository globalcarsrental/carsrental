Driving in Tokyo

If you plan to rent a car in Japan, familiarize yourself with local driving requirements and road conditions before your journey.

Important Things to Consider

  • Japan drives on the left side of the road.
  • Check the driving requirements applicable to your nationality.
  • Parking can be limited and expensive in central Tokyo.
  • Navigation is important when driving in a large metropolitan area.
  • Some highways use electronic toll systems.
  • Always follow Japanese road signs and local traffic regulations.

Is renting a car in Tokyo worth it?

Can tourists rent cars in Japan?

International visitors may be able to rent a car if they meet the applicable Japanese driving and rental requirements.

Do I need an international driving permit?

Requirements depend on your country and the documents recognized in Japan. Check the current requirements before traveling.

Where can I pick up a rental car in Tokyo?

Rental vehicles may be available at airport and city locations. Check the exact pickup location when comparing vehicles.

Is parking expensive in Tokyo?

Parking costs vary significantly by location. Central Tokyo can be more expensive than areas outside the city center.

Can I rent a car at Haneda Airport?
