Car Rental in Los Angeles

Los Angeles is a large and spread-out destination, making a rental car a convenient option for many travelers. Whether you are arriving at Los Angeles International Airport (LAX), visiting Hollywood, exploring Santa Monica or planning a California road trip, having access to a car can make getting around easier.

Before booking, compare available vehicles, rental companies, pickup locations, prices and rental conditions to find an option suitable for your trip.

Where to Rent a Car in Los Angeles

Los Angeles International Airport (LAX)

LAX is one of the most popular arrival points for visitors to Los Angeles. Comparing rental options before your flight can help you plan your transportation after landing.

Downtown Los Angeles

Downtown Los Angeles can be a convenient option for travelers who are already staying in the city and do not need an airport pickup.

Hollywood

Travelers staying around Hollywood can compare nearby rental options depending on availability and their itinerary.

Santa Monica and Beverly Hills

If your trip focuses on the Westside, comparing rental locations around Santa Monica or Beverly Hills may provide convenient alternatives.

Los Angeles Car Rental Tips

  • Compare several rental options before booking.
  • Check the pickup and return location carefully.
  • Compare the total rental cost rather than only the advertised daily rate.
  • Review fuel and mileage policies.
  • Check the rental company's insurance requirements.
  • Choose a vehicle that matches the number of passengers and luggage.
  • Consider parking costs when planning your Los Angeles budget.
  • Book early when possible, especially during busy travel periods.
  • Check the rental terms before confirming your reservation.

Choose the Right Rental Car

The best rental vehicle depends on your travel plans. Consider the following options:

Economy Cars

A practical option for travelers looking for a smaller vehicle and potentially lower rental costs.

SUVs

SUVs can provide additional passenger and luggage space and may be useful for families or California road trips.

Luxury Cars

Luxury vehicles can provide an upgraded experience for travelers who want additional comfort and style.

Convertibles

A convertible can be an attractive choice for travelers wanting to enjoy the California driving experience.

Compare Before Booking

Los Angeles Car Rental FAQ

How much does it cost to rent a car in Los Angeles?

Prices vary depending on the dates, vehicle, pickup location, rental company and availability. Comparing options can help you find a suitable rental.

Where is the best place to rent a car in Los Angeles?

The best location depends on your itinerary. LAX, Downtown Los Angeles and other city locations can provide different options.

Can I rent a car at LAX?

Yes. Travelers can compare rental car options serving Los Angeles International Airport.

Is it better to rent a car at LAX or in the city?

It depends on your itinerary and rental prices. Compare both airport and city pickup options before making your decision.

What type of car is best for Los Angeles?

Economy cars can work well for city travel, while SUVs may be more comfortable for families or longer trips.

Should I book a Los Angeles rental car in advance?

Booking ahead can give you more opportunity to compare available vehicles, locations and prices.
